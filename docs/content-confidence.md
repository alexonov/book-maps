# Content confidence — *The Shadow of the Wind* starter map

Honesty check for the pins in `src/data/locations.json` (opening + Days of Ashes 1–5). This is a reading companion, not a critical edition. Coordinates are WGS84. Blurbs are short originals, not quotations.

**Edition used for gating:** English part titles *The Cemetery of Forgotten Books*, then *Days of Ashes* chapters 1–5, split into beats in `src/data/beats.json`. Other printings number the opening differently.

**What “attested” means here:** the early text names the place, or a scene clearly happens there. **Inferred** means a walking-tour habit, a later volume, or a guess filled the gap.

## Routes

The four polylines are **inferred walking lines** between the pins above (Santa Anna → lower Rambla → Arc del Teatre; shop → Quatre Gats; shop → Ateneo; Plaça Reial → home). The novel does not list turn-by-turn streets.

## Left out on purpose

Fermín Romero de Torres, Nuria, Bea, and anyone else who has not been met by Days of Ashes 5. Paris scenes. Later Cemetery visits. Any “you will return here” copy.
